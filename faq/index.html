Stage 1 · Before you leave

01

Can I book yet?

First adventures are on their way, so be ready! Apply for early access and you'll hear first.

02

How much does it cost?

From €180 a day. That includes the car, the rooftop tent, a camp kitchen, your route, and endless adventure.

03

What licence do I need?

A standard driving licence. From outside the EU, you might need an International Driving Permit for local compliance.

04

Where do I pick up the car?

In the Barcelona area. Airport pick-up is available on request.

05

What is overlanding?

Travelling by 4x4 with everything you need on board, camping along the way. It's your travel, your activities and your hotel in one. Compared with a campervan, a 4x4 with a rooftop tent takes you further down the gravel and forest tracks.

06

When is the best time to go?

All year! Spain has a diverse climate, and its seasons open up different adventures.

Stage 2 · Out there

07

Do I need off-road experience?

No. It's soft-road: gravel, forest tracks and quiet mountain roads that any careful driver can handle.

08

How many people can it sleep?

Two fit in the rooftop tent, and the car seats five. Coming with more people? Extra tents are available on request.

09

Can I wild camp in Spain?

It's nuanced, and it depends on the local rules of the region.

10

What if something goes wrong?

Don't worry, we're available on WhatsApp. In an emergency, you have 24/7 roadside assistance.
