Bardenas Reales

Yes, Spain has a desert. Clay towers, gravel tracks and huge night skies.

  • Stargazing
  • Gravel tracks
  • Photography
  • Mountain biking

Why go

A semi-desert of clay, gypsum and sandstone in the south of Navarra, protected as a natural park and a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve. Wind and water have carved it into towers, flat-topped hills and dry gullies.

It feels like nowhere else in Europe, and it is driveable on gravel tracks.

What to do

Viewpoints and photography

  • Castildetierra

    The clay chimney everyone photographs, in the white heart of the park.

    Go an hour before sunset, when the towers start to glow. Stay on the track and keep your distance from the tower.

Gravel tracks

  • The Bardena Blanca loop

    A gravel loop through the white, most eroded part of the park.

    Vehicles may only use the marked tracks, and only during opening hours. A military firing range sits in the middle of the park and is off limits.

Stargazing

  • Night skies at the edge of the park

    Big, dark skies over flat land.

    The park closes at night, so stargaze from outside it, at a legal place to stay.

Mountain biking

  • Marked cycling routes

    Long gravel routes through the badlands.

    Take more water than you think, and avoid the hottest hours.

Best season

October to May. Summer days are very hot.
