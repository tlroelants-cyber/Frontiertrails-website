Sierra de Guara

Turquoise rivers, deep canyons and a medieval village on a cliff.

  • Canyoning
  • Kayaking
  • Via ferrata
  • Hiking

Why go

Sierra y Cañones de Guara Natural Park, in Huesca: limestone gorges cut by rivers, and one of the best-known canyoning areas in Europe.

Spend the day in the water, and the evening in a stone village.

What to do

Hiking

  • Alquézar and the Río Vero walkways

    A medieval village on a cliff, and a loop on boardwalks along the river gorge below it.

    Half a day. There may be a small fee in season.

Canyoning

  • The canyons of Guara

    Dozens of canyons, from easy water walks to technical descents.

    Go with a local guide unless you are experienced.

Climbing

  • Rodellar

    A small village surrounded by limestone overhangs, famous for sport climbing.

    Mostly hard routes. Best in summer, when the gorge stays cool.

Best season

Late spring to early autumn for canyoning. Spring and autumn for walking.
