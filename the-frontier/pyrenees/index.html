01 · The High Mountains

The Pyrenees

High peaks, mountain lakes and villages where the road runs out.

  • Hiking
  • Via ferrata
  • Climbing
  • Mountain biking

Why go

The Pyrenees are the mountain wall between Spain and France, topped by Aneto at 3,404 metres. On the Spanish side you get two national parks, high lakes, stone villages and valleys that empty out once you leave the main road.

Below the high peaks, the pre-Pyrenees add deep gorges and some of the darkest night skies in Catalonia.

What to do

Hiking

  • Ordesa valley and the Cola de Caballo

    The classic day walk in Ordesa y Monte Perdido National Park: up the valley through beech forest to the horsetail waterfall at the head of the canyon.

    A full day, long but not technical. In summer the valley is reached by shuttle bus from Torla.

  • Estany de Sant Maurici

    A mountain lake under the twin Encantats peaks, in Aigüestortes i Estany de Sant Maurici National Park.

    Easy walking around the lake. Private cars can only go so far, and park taxis run from Espot.

Gorges and kayaking

  • Congost de Mont-rebei

    A path cut into the wall of a gorge, high above a turquoise river. Kayaks go through it below.

    Half a day. Parking can need a reservation in busy periods.

Stargazing

  • Montsec

    A certified Starlight area in the pre-Pyrenees, known for its dark skies.

    Park, open the tent and look up. Clear, moonless nights are best.

Climbing and mountaineering

  • Aneto

    The highest peak of the Pyrenees, at 3,404 metres.

    A serious glacier climb: for experienced mountaineers, or with a guide.

Best season

June to October for the high trails. From late autumn to spring the high ground is under snow.
