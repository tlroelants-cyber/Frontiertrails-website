Rooftop tent vs campervan: which suits a Spain trip?

Both are great ways to see Spain. They suit different trips. Here’s the honest version.

4x4 with a rooftop tentCampervan
Where it can goA 4x4 handles gravel, forest tracks and steep, narrow mountain roads, and fits in village car parks.Best on paved roads. Gravel and narrow mountain roads get tricky, and old-town parking can be hard.
Setting upThe rooftop tent is up in about two minutes, with the bed already made.Nothing to set up: you sleep inside.
SleepingAbove the ground, with the view through the tent windows.Inside, out of the wind and the cold.
WeatherFine in rain and wind, but you’ll hear it. Cold nights mean a good sleeping bag.Warmer and quieter in bad weather.
CookingA camp kitchen outside, with the view.A kitchen inside, useful in the rain.
Where you can sleepAn open rooftop tent counts as camping, so you plan around campsites and authorised areas.Sleeping inside a properly parked van is allowed in more places.

Our take

If your trip is about cities and the coast, a campervan is hard to beat. If it’s about the landscapes in between, gravel tracks, mountain valleys, the desert, a 4x4 with a rooftop tent takes you further, and it’s simply more fun to drive.
