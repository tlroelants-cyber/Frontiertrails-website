The best time for a road trip in Spain, month by month

There’s no bad month for a trip from Barcelona, only the wrong landscape for the season. The trick is to follow the weather inland and uphill in summer, and down to the desert and the coast in winter.

WhenGo toKeep in mind
January–FebruaryThe desert and the outback: cold, clear days in Bardenas Reales and Los Monegros. La Garrotxa and quiet coast walks at Cap de Creus.The high Pyrenees are under snow.
March–AprilThe desert and outback at their best. Walking in Sierra de Guara. The coast wakes up.High trails are still snowbound.
MayAlmost everything opens: green valleys in the Pyrenees, the first canyoning in Guara, flowers in La Garrotxa.Snow can linger on the highest passes.
JuneThe high Pyrenees open up. Canyoning season in Guara. The sea gets warm enough to swim.
July–AugustPeak season for the Pyrenees and the canyons of Guara.The desert and outback are very hot. The coast is busiest in August.
SeptemberArguably the best month: the mountains are still open, the sea is warm and the crowds have gone.
OctoberAutumn colour in the Pyrenees, and the desert comes back into season.Early snow is possible up high.
November–DecemberBeech forests turn in La Garrotxa, the desert is quiet, coast walks are wild and empty.The high Pyrenees close for winter.

A rule of thumb

Summer: go up. Winter: go south and to the sea. Spring and autumn: anywhere, which is why they’re our favourite seasons.
