The open air

The car is loaded, the engine is humming, and the world begins to move.

With interesting chats, old songs on the aux, and snacks within reach, a rhythm can be found on the road. The tires move from pavement to gravel, and the time on the dashboard starts to matter less. Everything needed is already inside the car. Everything else is waiting somewhere out there in the distance.
