The night watch

There is something primitive in spending the night in nature. When camp is set and the sun drops, the world narrows down to the glow of the embers and the reach of the firelight.

The flame can be captivating: the light it shines, the heat it brings and the power it holds. As the fire dies down, the ceiling opens up to a sky full of stars. Returning to the basics makes one feel truly alive.
