The Mountain Kingdom

Crossing into Lesotho feels like stepping back in time. It is a world where men on horseback are draped in Basotho blankets, moving across the ridges like shadows of the past.

The homesteads are built from mother earth herself, sand, soil and dung, blending into the slopes of the Maloti Mountains. Here the ancient ways are still the primary pulse.

SPECS: Shot on Canon Prima AF-7, 35mm Kodak Gold
